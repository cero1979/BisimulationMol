## Final tradeoff

Seventeen pages are within the instruction's acceptable 16--17 page range and
would imply two pages beyond the stated free allowance if publisher pagination
is unchanged. The final reduction retains the fixed-point theorem and proof,
all required propositions and counterexamples, the benchmark, public GINsim
controls, HPN-DREAM/CASPOTS analysis, and both GIM Petri-net diagrams. The
scaling experiment remains numerically described in the manuscript and fully
available as a plot and machine-readable table in the repository.

Further reduction to 15 pages would require moving a proof or a principal
validation/application figure out of the article. That tradeoff is not
recommended before editorial feedback.
