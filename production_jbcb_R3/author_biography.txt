Carlos Ramirez Ovalle is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Natural Sciences and Mathematics at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana Cali, Colombia, and a member of the Statistics and Applied Mathematics research group (EMAP). He obtained his undergraduate degree in Mathematics in 2005 and his Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences in 2016. His research interests include mathematical logic, semantics of linear logic, fixed-point methods for two-player games, and formal verification of hybrid systems. He has also participated in research applying topology and graph theory to displacement phenomena and process optimization.
